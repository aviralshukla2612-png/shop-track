## 3. Full / Long Description (Max 4000 characters)

**Shop Track** is an all-in-one retail store, stock valuation, and business management OS tailored specifically for FMCG retailers, kirana stores, bakery shops, and snack merchants.

Run your retail store with full financial clarity, eliminate stockouts, track gross margins, and access an authentic offline Master Snack Catalogue.

---

### 🌟 Key Highlights & Features:

#### 📦 Real-Time Inventory & Valuation
* **Live Store Valuation**: Instantly view Total Cost Basis, Potential Retail Value, Total Units in Stock, and Total Gross Margin.
* **Low Stock Alerts**: Automatic reorder triggers so popular items are never out of stock.
* **Batch Inward & Stock Adjustments**: Easily record new delivery shipments, damaged stock write-offs, or manual adjustments.

#### 📖 Offline Master Product Catalogue
* **Pre-Loaded Snack Database**: Over 80+ verified Indian snack products with packaging images, MRPs, barcodes, pack sizes, and categories (Balaji Wafers, Gopal Snacks, Real Bites, and more).
* **1-Tap Add to Shop Inventory**: Stock wholesale products into your inventory with custom cost and retail selling prices in seconds.
* **Barcode & Keyword Search**: Rapidly filter by brand, subcategory (wafers, namkeen, bakery, cookies), or barcode.

#### 🛒 Purchase & Procurement Management
* **Procurement Shopping List**: Keep track of required reorders and pending supplier deliveries.
* **Supplier Directory**: Manage wholesale distributors with purchase balances and contact records.

#### 📊 Financial Analytics & COGS Profit
* **Gross Profit Breakdown**: Daily, weekly, monthly, and all-time revenue and margin calculation.
* **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)**: Know your true unit-by-unit profitability after supplier costs.
* **Top Performing Products**: Rank bestsellers by revenue, sales volume, and gross profit.

#### 📑 PDF Reports & Financial Statements
* **1-Tap PDF Export**: Generate printable Inventory Valuation Statements, Supplier Inward Receipts, and Expense Audits.
* **Instant Thermal / Share**: Export PDF reports directly via WhatsApp, Email, or Print.

#### 💰 Operating Expense Tracking
* **Store Overheads**: Record rent, electricity, packaging carry bags, and maintenance expenses to calculate your true Net Profit.

#### 🔒 Offline-First & Private
* **100% Offline SQLite Architecture**: Your store data stays secure on your device without requiring internet access.
* **Clean Data Control**: Reset or backup your store database anytime from Settings.
