## 1. Introduction & Core Philosophy
**Shop Track** ("we", "our", or "us") provides a retail inventory, snack product catalogue, procurement tracking, and financial valuation application for FMCG retail stores, kirana shops, and business owners.

We believe that your business transactions, product margins, supplier contacts, and sales figures are strictly **confidential and proprietary to your business**. Therefore, Shop Track is engineered from the ground up as an **offline-first application**. We do not collect, monetize, or transmit your commercial data to external cloud servers.

---

## 2. Information We Handle (100% Stored Locally On-Device)
When you utilize Shop Track, the following business information is recorded and stored **exclusively within an encrypted/sandboxed SQLite database on your local device**:

* **Store Profile & Setup**: Store name, owner name, business address, and GST/Tax ID (if provided).
* **Inventory & Valuation**: Stock quantities, item purchase costs, retail selling prices, pack sizes, barcodes, and minimum stock alerts.
* **Sales Transactions & Invoices**: Daily transaction totals, items sold, unit margins, and payment modes (Cash, UPI, Credit).
* **Purchase Orders & Procurement**: Inward stock records, purchase orders, and shopping procurement lists.
* **Supplier Directory**: Supplier names, contact telephone numbers, and outstanding balance ledgers.
* **Operating Expenses**: Categorized store overheads (rent, electricity, packaging bags, transport).

> ⚠️ **None of this data is ever synced, transmitted, or accessible to us or any third party.**

---

## 3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used
Shop Track requests minimal Android/device permissions, strictly required for offline functionality:

| Permission | Purpose & Scope |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Storage / Media Access** | Required exclusively when you choose to export PDF reports (Inventory Valuation statements, Purchase lists) or CSV spreadsheets to your device's Downloads folder. |
| **Camera (Optional)** | Used solely to scan product barcodes locally on your device for rapid item lookup. No photos or video feeds are captured, recorded, or sent anywhere. |
| **Internet Access (Optional)** | Used solely to fetch publicly hosted product packaging imagery for the verified master snack catalogue or display web-based resources. |

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing
* **Zero Third-Party Ad Networks**: Shop Track contains no advertisements, banner networks, or ad tracking SDKs (e.g., no AdMob, Unity, or Facebook Audience Network).
* **Zero Behavioral Tracking**: We do not use third-party user tracking, profiling, or behavioral monitoring tools.
* **No Data Brokering**: We will never sell, lease, trade, or distribute your private store data to market researchers, wholesalers, or competitors.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Full Deletion Control (User Rights)
You maintain 100% ownership and control over all business data entered into Shop Track:
* **Instant In-App Data Reset**: You can permanently purge all stored inventory, sales, purchases, and expenses anytime by navigating to:  
  `Settings -> Store Data Management -> Reset All Store Data`.
* **Complete Uninstallation**: Uninstalling the Shop Track app from your Android device immediately and permanently destroys all local database records, cached images, and user preferences stored by the app.
