Privacy Policy for Shop Track

Application: Shop Track: Retail Inventory • Package: com.businesstracker.app.business_tracker • Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction & Privacy Commitment

Shop Track ("we", "our", or "us") provides retail inventory management, stock valuation, FMCG master catalogues, and financial tracking software.

We respect the confidentiality of merchants and small business owners. Your pricing, profit margins, supplier contracts, and sales records are your proprietary commercial secrets. Shop Track is built on strict offline-first principles: your records stay on your local device.

🔒 Zero Cloud Tracking: We do not collect, transmit, store, or monetize your store's sales, inventory records, or customer contact details on remote cloud servers.

2. Data Handled Strictly On-Device

All data entered into Shop Track is stored exclusively within your local Android SQLite database:

  • Store Profile: Shop name, owner name, business address, and GST/Tax ID.
  • Inventory & Valuation: Stock quantities, unit costs, selling prices, barcodes, pack sizes, and low stock thresholds.
  • Sales Transactions: Invoices, payment modes (Cash, UPI), items sold, and gross margins.
  • Procurement & Orders: Shopping lists, supplier delivery receipts, and pending purchase orders.
  • Suppliers: Wholesale vendor names, phone numbers, and balances.
  • Operating Expenses: Store overhead records (rent, electricity, carry bags, transport).

3. Device Permissions & Usage

Permission How & When It Is Used
Storage / Media Access Used exclusively when you choose to export PDF reports (Valuation statements, Purchase orders) or CSV spreadsheets to your device's Downloads directory.
Camera (Optional) Used strictly for on-device barcode scanning to instantly locate products. No photos or video recordings are saved or uploaded.
Internet Access (Optional) Used solely to load verified public product images for the offline packaged snack catalogue.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

  • No Ads: Shop Track contains zero third-party advertising networks (no AdMob, Facebook Ads, or banner tracking).
  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not embed behavioral tracking or telemetry SDKs.
  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, rent, or share your commercial data with third parties.

5. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Instant Reset: You can clear all store records at any time directly in the app via Settings → Store Data Management → Reset All Store Data.
  • App Uninstall: Removing the app from your device automatically purges all locally stored databases, caches, and preferences.
